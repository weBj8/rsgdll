Garry's Mod's lua_shared (linux64 and linux32, server and client) is built with LUA_IDSIZE=128, not stock LuaJIT's 60. `lua_getstack` stores i_ci at 0xb8 on x86-64 and 0xa4 on x86, so lua_Debug is 192 bytes on 64-bit and 168 on 32-bit. A stock-sized 120-byte record caused a SIGSEGV in lua_getlocal under srcds. To verify: `objdump -d lua_shared.so | awk '/<lua_getstack>:/,/ret/'`. rsgdll-sys `LUA_IDSIZE` is now 128, which is also large enough for stock LuaJIT tests. Windows DLLs were not checked.
